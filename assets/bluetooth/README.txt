Realtek Bluetooth firmware for USB adapters with an RTL8761B or RTL8761C
(RTL8761BU, RTL8761CU), from linux-firmware (rtl_bt/), unmodified:
https://git.kernel.org/pub/scm/linux/kernel/git/firmware/linux-firmware.git
Porpoise uploads it to such an adapter when it starts it (porpoise_bt.cpp).
Redistributable under LICENCE.rtlwifi_firmware.txt (Realtek Semiconductor Corp.).
